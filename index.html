Bio

I'm a Research Scientist in the Department of Radiology at the University of Pennsylvania, where I develop medical image analysis software and AI-based tools that move from research prototypes into real-world clinical use. I hold a Ph.D. in Bioengineering from the University of Pennsylvania and a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Case Western Reserve University. My work spans neuroimaging, traumatic brain injury, and cloud-based deep learning platforms for analyzing abdominal CT and other medical images, with contributions to widely used open-source toolkits including ITK and ANTs (Advanced Normalization Tools). I'm an author on more than 100 peer-reviewed publications and hold a published patent application for a medical image analysis platform.
